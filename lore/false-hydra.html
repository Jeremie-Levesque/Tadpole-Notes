False Hydra

The False Hydra was a memory-eating creature that nested in the sewers beneath Drusburgh, the source of the city's vanishing people. Its song erased all memory of those it devoured, so that whole families disappeared and no one could recall they had ever existed. The party hunted and killed it during their time in Drusburgh.

Nature

The False Hydra feeds unseen: its droning "singing" — first a faint lullaby behind the walls, then a hum in the very stones — overwrites the minds of those who hear it, deleting the memory of each victim it takes. Townsfolk stared blankly at anyone who asked after the missing, as if the names had dissolved on their tongues. Tessa, a ship's quartermaster, recorded the phenomenon in a warning letter before she herself was taken, urging the party to "search beneath the city… something feeds there" and to "follow the song."[1]

Lair

It denned in the damaged, collapsing sewers under Drusburgh, a state of disrepair that mirrored the decay of the city above. The tunnels were littered with humanoid and animal bones, and the potholes and cave-ins in the streets matched the remains below. The party tracked the music to an open nest strewn with corpses, where they noticed there were no living animals anywhere in the sewers.[2]

Encounter

Even in its lair the creature clouded the party's minds — they briefly forgot why they had come. When it finally emerged they fought and killed it. With the False Hydra dead, its hold on the town broke and the forgotten dead were remembered at last; the meddling of Old Mother Salt proved to be a separate matter.[3] Returning to the surface, the townsfolk were struck with grief as they realized how many people they had forgotten.

Aftermath

Eldil severed several of the creature's heads as proof. Strangely, Dryzfrin was able to recover six tadpoles from inside them, tying the monster to the wider illithid infestation.[4] The party brought the heads to Darius Veynar of the Crowns guard to claim a reward and to report what they had learned of the creature, the still-missing children, and the witch possibly linked to them.
