The Tadpole

The illithid tadpole is the parasite implanted in each member of The Party aboard the Crashed Nautiloid, and the object the whole campaign is named for. Rather than triggering the usual rapid ceremorphosis into a mind flayer, these tadpoles behave abnormally: they linger, communicate with their hosts, and offer power instead of immediately consuming them.

Origin

The party was infected while captive on the Crashed Nautiloid, waking with the parasites already in their heads.[1] The ship's artifact and the presence of allied intellect devourers suggest the tadpoles were engineered for a purpose beyond ordinary illithid reproduction.

Abnormal Nature

Unlike a normal infection, these tadpoles do not quickly transform their hosts. Halsin examines the party and notes the tadpole is "not normal" and is surrounded by Netherese magic, a forbidden branch of the weave created by Karsus and outlawed by Mystra.[2] Gale later confirms the tadpole problem is widespread far beyond Longtooth Island, and suspects every host may be meant to transform together in a single event.[3] The Netherese enchantment protecting the parasite ties it directly to the plot of the Absolute.

Powers and Dreams

The tadpole speaks to its host in dreams, offering strength, "vengeance," and new abilities in exchange for acceptance or for consuming additional tadpoles. Nearly every member of The Party has been tempted this way:

  • Eldil repeatedly eats tadpoles he finds and hides them from the group, guided by a voice urging "one of us, one of us."[4]
  • Dryzfrin is offered powers such as Horrific Visage and, later, the ability to bend others' wills "for their own good," which he chooses to suppress.[5]
  • Jaden and Grimgil are marked in their flesh and offered "true tongue of the wild" and dominion over deformed, tentacled beasts.[6]

Accepting the offers grants real power but advances the host toward ceremorphosis.

Ceremorphosis

Ceremorphosis is the process by which a tadpole consumes and rewrites its host into a mind flayer: the body is remade, memories are erased and replaced with mind-flayer thought, and the newborn illithid answers the call of an elder brain. The party has seen the result in the mind flayers dug from the ground near the Crashed Nautiloid, and in Benjamin Caldwell, a Celadon survivor who collapsed and sprouted tentacles days after his rescue — "a classic type of ceremorphosis."[7] Because the party's parasites are abnormal (see Abnormal Nature above), their own transformation has been delayed — something even Halsin finds strange.[8] The threat is dramatized in Eldil's nightmare, in which he undergoes full ceremorphosis — skin greying, memories dissolving, hearing the elder brain — and wakes with white eyes and a missing fingernail.[9]

True Souls

Hosts of these special tadpoles are recognized by the Absolute's cult as True Souls. Minthara and other cultists urge the party to "turn to the Absolute" and stop resisting, treating them as chosen rather than as prey.[10] Grimgil is identified as a True Soul early on and reported as such to Celadon.[11]

Removal

Two possible cures have been raised:

  • A Zaith'isk at the nearby Gith Crèche, a githyanki device said to remove tadpoles.[12]
  • Nettie provides vials meant to be drunk if a host begins to visibly transform.[13]
