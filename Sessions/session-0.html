Session 0

  • Players wake in the nautiloid ship, they get infected and pass out.
  • Players wake again, the ship, that is a living thing, is on fire.
  • Devils entered the room where the players were and starting attacking them
  • Players hear from the tadpole in their head that they need to get to the cockpit and activate the realm jump function of the ship.
  • They also had the opportunity to free an intellect devourer from a corpse. Grimgil ate it?
  • Aeson drank a random blue potion and became blind.
  • While players are making their way, they fought a dragon that was badly injured and chained to the deck of the nautiloid
  • Grimgil got hit by an intellect devourer ability that reduced his intelligence to 0. Players solved a riddle to create a potion for Grimgil restoring his intelligence.
  • After that, players made their way to the cabin and found a mind flayer fighting with a cambion named Commander Zhalk.
  • They made it to realm jump, and activated it.
  • After the realm jump, the ship was so damaged that it crashed on an island. The players were thrown off the ship and fell with debris. When they were about to hit the ground, they started slowing down and levitating before being laid on the beach.
  • Next session, players get woken up by Eldil. They find an artifact lying on the ground.
  • They made their way through the nautiloid ship and helped 4 intellect devourers in a teleportation circle made specifically for them.
  • After that they heard cries of help. When rushing to the aid of civilians digging something out of the ground, they notice the civilians are digging a mind flayer. When they are spotted by the mind flayer, the civilians turn on the players and fight. The players killed the mind flayer and the civilians.
  • They get out of the ship and meet Gale. Who seemed to have just teleported here using a teleportation circle. They notice Gale is infected like them and he points them into the direction of chapel ruins where artifacts are hidden. He tells them that he will meet them again soon and hopes they can share in the hidden treasures of the chapel ruins and that they will be on friendly terms.
  • Players make their way to the ruins to find Shadowheart trying to enter through the front door but finding it locked and not able to open it. She asks if they’ve seen an artifact lying around. Makir showed it to her. She compelled it to come back to her, but found out that it had attuned to the players. After having a difficult interaction with the players, she tells them to bring The Artifact to Baldur’s Gate. And that she will be waiting for them there.
  • By doing a dc 10 Religion check, players notice Shadowheart wears the symbol of the goddess of Shar.
  • Shar is the Faerûnian goddess of darkness and night, the malevolent twin counterpart to the goddess of the moon, Selûne. (pour André – Shar took pleasure in corrupting Aasimar in various ways to mainly create discord in their ranks).
  • Players then decided to go up the side of the cliff to find bandits looting around the upper part of the chapel. After an encounter, the players rid the place of the bandits on the outside. Eldil and Aeson left them during the night. Eldil was feeling torn by more important things; he grew out his Aasimar wings and flew away. Aeson left without talking much, just saying his objectives didn’t seem to align with those of the group.
  • Players enter the chapel the next day to find more bandits inside. They fight and kill them.
  • They then solve a puzzle leading back to the lower levels of the chapel through a secret corridor.
  • They find a room with skeletons in it and signs of battle. After killing the risen skeletons, the players can examine the statue of Jergal in the middle of the room. They notice it's supposed to be holding a tome in its hands. Makir placed the tome which opened another secret door.
  • In that room was the most beautiful and ornate sarcophagus the players had ever seen. The sarcophagus opened by itself and out came a high priest of Jergal. The high priest informs the players of Jergal’s history and tells them their paths will cross again later, because their destinies will impact the things that are to come. He also gifted the players of his treasures.
  • Jergal : was originally the god of Death. Is now the lord of the end of everything. He grew wary of his throne. Hence he decided to gift its empty powers to the dead three (Murder, Tyranny and Death). Balg used to be an assassin and a charmer. He took over the portfolio of Murders. Bane used to be a warrior and a tyrant. He took over the portfolio of tyranny. Finally, Myrkul used to be a necromancer. He took over the portfolio of death.
