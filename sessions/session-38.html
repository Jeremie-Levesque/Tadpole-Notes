Session 38

  • [0:01:00] The session opens on the aftermath of Grimgil's reincarnation: reborn a small, thin, green-tinged dwarf, he spends the first hours vacant and vomiting — his mind "still loading" — before his old self finally resurfaces in the unfamiliar body.
  • [0:07:47] Still guests of Gustave Marquet, the party turns to housekeeping — resolving to forge a clean new identity for the wanted Bow, while Jaden, who can now wear the face of Gustave's dead daughter "Evelyn", quietly settles with him whether to walk the world as her; they buy quality paper for the forgery.
  • [0:22:05] With the king Aldric Stormborn — an Absolute leader and Bow's true father — due back from the north within the week, they resolve to intercept him on the road before he reaches Bruxar, and weigh recruiting the Gilded Fangs as allies against the crown.
  • [0:37:33] Having bought a scrying focus, Grimgil scries on Therian through a letter written in his hand: the vision shows Bow's adoptive father alive but battered and bloodied, riding under guard among soldiers bearing the Absolute's tabard, moving south along the road.
  • [1:18:35] A second scrying — keyed to the king's likeness from a coin, finds Aldric lounging in a purple pavilion on a makeshift throne, his caravan only about two days out.
  • [1:24:38] Scouting ahead in animal form, Grimgil spots their encampment barely half a day away
  • [1:34:30] The plan takes shape — slip into the escort camp by night to spirit Therian out first, then decide whether to risk a strike at the king — though they worry their shared tadpoles might let him feel them coming.
  • [2:01:50] Cloaked in his own invisibility, Jaden steals into the escort camp, finds the prisoner's tent, and reaches into Therian's mind — naming himself a friend of Thimithiel and offering escape; when the wary prisoner balks, he produces Bow's own letter to earn his trust.
  • [2:14:35] A guard interrupts and beats the prisoner, so Jaden tries to hold him with a spell, it fails so he tries to bluff the soldier while wearing a stolen face, then grabs Therian and Dimension Doors the two of them clear of the camp with his cape.
  • [2:20:53] The escape proves a trap: a swirling vortex the king had bound to Therian erupts, Dryz's counterspell fails, and everyone beside is dragged through it — deposited squarely inside the king's throne-tent.
  • [2:26:00] The king removes his crown and produces a black stone veined with violet light — a Netherstone, one of three forged by Karsus. Each empowers a vessel of the Dead Three: death and decay through Gortash (Myrkul), murder and blood through Orin (Bhaal), and tyranny through the king himself.
  • [2:28:21] Karsus needed something greater, he explains. An Elder Brain bent on godhood: every tadpole is a seed and every transformed victim a worshipper feeding its power. The smuggled shipments through Grey Harbour, Drusburgh, Caldaren, Springvale and Bruxar have quietly seeded Ardezax as a staging ground for an invasion of Baldur’s Gate.
  • [2:30:54] As for why he has hunted Bow: the god of tyranny can suffer no heir, no bloodline to promise a future. Thimithiel's death is to be his "final offering" — and he declares there is no king left here to kill, "only a god waiting to be born."
  • [2:40:31] Battle erupts. The party sets the pavilion ablaze, Dryz pins the king with telekinesis and, Eldil with slow, and Jaden pours poisoned arrows into him while Grimgil summons a beast — but the king answers with legendary might, Absolute priests and his honor guard, and as the fight turns desperate Bow renounces his throne and his claim to the bloodline.
  • [3:26:36] Then the cruelest turn: the king reveals that Therian himself has been half-transformed into an illithid, and seizes control of him — driving the rescued father, tentacle and blade, against his own son Bow.
  • [3:32:01] With the pavilion burning and the king already swelling toward a second form, the session breaks mid-battle. To be continued.
