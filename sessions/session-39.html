Session 39

  • [0:14:25] As the king Aldric Stormborn swells into his second form, only Grimgil's religion check catches what appears on the battlefield: three black hands bearing the holy symbol of Bane, god of tyranny.
  • [0:18:31] The fight resumes in the burning pavilion. The king barks "Stop standing there, attack!" at his soldiers; Dryz quickens Synaptic Static and locks him in telekinesis, Eldil smites and Grimgil's Wildfire Spirit sets a banner alight — but the king teleports free of the hold and drags his own guards between himself and the party's blows.
  • [0:36:30] The hands of Bane make the king's authority physical: one smothers the area in magical silence, and with his Tyrant's Decree he singles out Jaden, Grimgil and Dryz — "This has nothing to do with you. Stay out of this." — rooting Jaden in place. Drawing on his purple crystal, he later conjures another black gauntlet: "It's time to lay down the law."
  • [0:52:16] Restrained in the tentacles of his half-transformed father, Bow will not shoot Therian — "even if he's half transformed, potentially far gone, I still have hope" — even as Therian turns his illithid attacks on his own son.
  • [1:10:15] Bow drops under the soldiers' blades. Dryz's Healing Word is counterspelled, so he subtly counterspells the counterspell to get him back up, and Grimgil heals him again — but Bow keeps falling.
  • [1:18:56] Eldil lands two massive smites — some 180 damage — without touching the king: while four banners stand, he can throw a guard into harm's way twice a round. The king turns on him in return — "You have been a nuisance in my side for a long time" — and cuts him down.
  • [1:37:36] Jaden rises from the floor, sees his Healing Word on Bow countered, then scores a natural 20 with the Oath Bow for 59 damage — and the king pulls a guard into the arrow's path.
  • [1:41:49] With Bow unconscious in Therian's tentacles, the king sets his sword against his son's chest and drives it through: Thimithiel is dead. Dryz screams and twins a Psychic Lance into father and king, stunning Therian.
  • [1:54:44] Jaden fires again; the king hurls his last servant into the shot, and in that half-second of distraction Jaden's second arrow takes him behind the ear. His sword falls from his hand; he reaches for the arrow — "No, I was finally there" — and falls dead, face first.
  • [1:59:09] His inner circle, who knew every working of the cult, cannot fathom one of the Absolute's three leaders dying — and fight on. Dryz lifts Bow's body into the bag of holding with telekinesis, and Grimgil polymorphs the nearly dead Eldil into a T-Rex.
  • [2:07:38] Therian clutches his head and sinks to his knees beside his son's vanishing body, weeping; a round later Grimgil's wolf and Booming Blade cut him down.
  • [2:08:51] A tadpole compulsion seizes Jaden and Dryz: they cannot leave without the king's tadpoles. Jaden loots the purple crystal and the crown, Dryz rips the king's head off with telekinesis, and Grimgil Misty Steps over to claim the king's magic sword for Eldil.
  • [2:26:52] With the last of the faithful dead, they flee: the party turns invisible — Dryz under Greater Invisibility — Grimgil becomes a giant eagle bearing two of them, and all slip out into the woods unseen.
  • [2:30:30] Within the minute, Grimgil revivifies Bow with mud, spit and 300 gold of diamond dust. Bow wakes gasping, screaming at his father the king that he will never accept him as a father — his mouth full of a hairy dwarf's mud.
  • [2:34:35] Jaden means to tell his father Marquet that a new king must be installed — Bow, if he wants it, Marquet himself, or by election. When Grimgil wonders at the pair who grabbed the crystal and the tadpoles, Jaden calls the crown simple proof of the king's death, and Dryz smoothly lies that the tadpoles must be kept out of the wrong hands.
  • [2:37:36] A couple of days from town, they slip an hour off the road and camp for a long rest. Felling one of the big bosses levels the party to 14, and Grimgil's Detect Magic reads the loot: the crown is enchantment — it "snares the senses and bewitches the mind" — the sword is magical, and the purple gem is a Netherstone of Netherese magic.
  • [2:43:27] Dryz begins the hour-long attunement to the crown — time for the others to talk him out of it — and his wriggling pocket gives away the tadpoles he extracted from the king, ordinary ones like their own. He argues for keeping a live specimen for a cure; Grimgil offers an insect vial for one sample and to destroy the rest.
  • [2:45:05] Fighting the pull like Bilbo with the Ring, Dryz beats a Wisdom save at disadvantage — helped by Eldil's aura — drops the tadpoles face down and turns away, and Grimgil gingerly stows them in his pack. To be continued in a month.
