Session 35

  • [0:06:37] Picking up mid-fight from last session, Grimgil returns as a giant eagle to find the rest of the party bloodied and near death in the Caldwell farmhouse; Eldil burns Aura of Vitality and Lay on Hands to haul everyone back to their feet as the last attackers fall.
  • [0:11:12] Still in the ruined farmhouse in the dead of night with no long rest, the party debates the two surviving children and concludes it may be drawing the transformations itself — "we're the danger here" — and leaves the kids hidden with Mrs. Caldwell rather than take them. A short walk through the village confirms the worst: the attackers were the villagers themselves, partially and unevenly transformed into illithids and brain-like devourers, several now dead.
  • [0:22:22] Back at Bow's repaired house they check on Clara — still herself, but with a residue of white crusted around her mouth and grown "about an inch taller."
  • [0:25:31] Grimgil casts speak with animals and tries to hire a night-watch owl with jerky, but the deal falls through and the owl flies off. The party lights a fire, sets watches, and finally takes its long rest; come morning it brews tea from the plants Grimgil's plant-growth ritual revived, then heads into Springvale to learn why illithids keep erupting wherever it goes.
  • [0:44:19] The "investigation" is quick: villagers report no outside attackers — the transformations came from within. Eldil's detect evil and good pings an aberration signature on a closed crate at a market stall.
  • [0:53:38] The crate matches the hag's smuggling crates, and something is moving inside. Grimgil casts reduce and stuffs it into the bag of holding while wary villagers eye them as thieves and the party spots wanted posters of Bow; Bow agrees they will pose as bounty-and-captive if the guards close in.
  • [1:04:56] Weighing its three leads — the Absolute cult (their only tadpole connection), the hag's crates, and Bow's missing father Therian — the party settles on the king's city of Bruxar, some seven days east, where corrupt king Aldric Stormborn (Bow's biological father and an Absolute leader) reigns and may also hold Therian.
  • [1:17:37] Grimgil ritual-casts divination, asking the earth whether they will find Therian in Bruxar; the omen is cryptic — they might not find what they seek even if they find him: lied to, dead, or corrupted.
  • [1:26:07] Clara chooses to stay behind and keep Bow's farmhouse rather than adventure. With no memory of home or family beyond her fellow captives, she and Jaden — who knew her in the hag's cages — share a quiet, conflicted goodbye.
  • [1:35:52] On the road Jaden has Grimgil send an animal-messenger letter to Marquet, the king's right hand and a hoped-for ally, to be answered once they draw near Bruxar.
  • [1:43:13] By the third day of travel Jaden, Bow, and Eldil fall ill — exhausted, half-speed, disadvantage on everything — while Grimgil, who alone escaped the frog-creatures' stuns, stays healthy. Nothing they try (detect magic, lesser restoration, remove disease, foraging, repeated divinations) touches it.
  • [1:54:52] They finally open the reduced crate: rotten fish sprouting tentacles, corrupted and undead, stamped from a fishing outfit out of Grey Harbour. Burning it with wall of fire leaves a medallion bearing the Absolute's insignia in the ash — tying the tadpole plague to Absolute-run fish shipments.
  • [2:06:35] A fresh divination reveals the sickness is "eating them from the inside out." Piecing it together, the three realize the farm's stunning frog-creatures infected them during the fight — a second kind of tadpole layered over their Netherese-touched ones.
  • [2:09:31] Reaching Bruxar, they find the king's city locked down under the Absolute: temples closed, the paranoid king gone with hundreds of Crown's guards, food short and the slums restless. Grimgil slips inside as a bird, then a cat, and hires Silverwick — a cleric of Selûne worshipping in secret — to treat the dying party outside the walls, shaking off slum thieves by claiming rabies and, once, by becoming a massive dire wolf.
  • [2:39:53] Silverwick's own divination pins the cure to remove curse. Over the casting he fills them in on Bruxar: the Absolute's grip on the closed temples runs all the way to the throne, the king has grown stricter and more selfish and left the city with a couple hundred Royal Guards, and hungry unrest in the slums is being quashed fast.
  • [2:56:14] Silverwick lifts the curse — the implanted aberration eggs die before their twenty-four-hour hatch, which would otherwise have killed each host and birthed an illithid. Cured but drained and near-broke, the party resolves to lie low in Bruxar. To be continued.
