  • [1:32:57] Flush with gold, the party resupplies in Bruxar — Jaden commissions grappling arrows (iron-tipped with a rope loop for scaling walls) and finer +1 arrows from a blacksmith.

  • [1:55:15] Pressed on the betrayal, Marquet calls it a mistake he regrets — but Dryz's insight (a natural 20) reads the regret as hollow: he is sorry mainly that the trade never brought his true daughter back.

He names Ketheric Thorm of Baldur’s Gate among the cult's three leaders — alongside Aldric Stormborn and Orin — and places the king at Caldaren, expected back within the week coming from the north.
  • [2:24:58] In a quieter moment Marquet admits he later went back to find the hag and undo the trade but failed, confirms what Jaden is, and offers to be his father again "if she wants." As his wife and now-grown children return home for a tense reunion, Jaden lingers, unsure whether to stay. To be continued.
