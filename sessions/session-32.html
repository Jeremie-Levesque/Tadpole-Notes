Session 32

  • [0:04:57] the party recaps last session: they beat the children and the sewn-mouth woman (Clara), searched the hut, descended into the hag's underground workshop, and identified the children's-laughter music box as her anathema. Jaden stands in the salt circle holding the box, waiting for Dryz to begin the three-round ritual, while Eldil recalls his private end-of-session vision of Manuil taking a religious symbol and shattering it.
  • [0:12:16] Combat resumes at the top of initiative. The hag floods the room as a swarm of identical copies, and only one is real — the party has to shoot through the illusions to find her, each arrow passing harmlessly through a false image before it vanishes.
  • [0:17:22] The hag casts hypnotic pattern, charming and incapacitating Dryz and Jaden (speed zero) until someone wakes them or they take damage; Bow fires to snap them out.
  • [0:25:13] The hag lashes out with spectral claws that knock party members prone for heavy damage, and curses those she marks with her Witch Strike — disadvantage on everything and no reactions until the curse ends.
  • [0:44:53] A counterspell duel breaks out: the hag counterspells Dryz's counterspell, so he answers with a quickened psychic lance and a twinned firebolt to keep the pressure on.
  • [1:09:13] The hag's tongue-twister curse lands on Grimgil: he can no longer cast spells with verbal components, and everything he says comes out as its opposite — his command to his wolf to "attack" leaves it doing nothing at all.
  • [1:13:01] The hag hits terrifyingly hard, one area effect dealing 42 damage; Grimgil, Jaden, and later Bow are all knocked unconscious, and the fight teeters on the edge of a total-party wipe.
  • [1:15:56] the party realizes they must not kill her before the ritual finishes or she will simply return, so they focus on keeping everyone standing — Grimgil's goodberries and healing words — while Dryz holds the three rounds of casting.
  • [1:27:37] Bloodied, the hag offers a truce — "I still have things to offer you... let's stop this nonsense, we're fighting the same enemy" — but Bow refuses and looses an arrow.
  • [1:29:02] Bow's arrow passes clean through her mouth; the real hag collapses into a cloud of dust and lingering mist that tries to flee.
  • [1:32:41] Dryz completes the ritual: the fleeing mist is dragged back into the music box, and box and mist crumble to dust together — Old Mother Salt is dead for good. As she dies, her half-sunken house begins to sink into the swamp.
  • [1:37:31] Racing the rising water, the party grabs a desk of scattered notes (possible intel), frees the caged children — Grimgil smashing locks, Jaden picking others — and carries out the two unconscious captives and Clara. Grimgil shifts into a polar bear to haul people and clear falling rubble.
  • [1:44:16] With the hag dead, the magic sealing Clara's sewn mouth breaks; Jaden wakes her with a goodberry. She is terrified and disbelieving, certain it is another of the hag's tricks.
  • [1:48:29] Out on the shore as the house sinks, Jaden reveals himself as the boy who once escaped and gently tells Clara it is finally over. Grimgil takes the form of a white warhorse to carry the freed children, and Jaden persuades Clara to follow the party out of the woods.
  • [1:57:54] the party travels a day to the forest's edge, recovers their five horses, and makes camp; the woods already feel less cursed, with distant birdsong returning. Around the fire Eldil and Jaden share a rare emotional moment about what comes next.
  • [2:04:40] Plans take shape: eventually kill the corrupt king Aldric Stormborn of Ardezax, get intel on his advisor Marquet (a hoped-for ally), and hunt Eldil's brother Manuil — though the deal meant to reveal Manuil's location only bought "half a soul for half an answer." Their main goal remains returning to Baldur’s Gate to get the tadpoles out of their heads. Jaden plans to send an Animal Messenger letter to his father Gustave in the morning, and the party debates where to bring the rescued children — perhaps the Temple of Selûne in Drusburgh — worried that many were sold by their own parents.
  • [2:20:38] Through the night watches Bow and Jaden notice Clara's body slowly changing — growing taller and lankier, taking on the shape of the hag's true form — while the fading mark on their necks still prickles and burns. Jaden wonders if she could be the same race as him. The party resolves to try dispelling whatever is on her in the morning as they take a long rest and level up. To be continued.
