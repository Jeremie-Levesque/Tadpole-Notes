Session 33

  • [0:05:03] Morning at camp after the long rest. Overnight Clara kept shifting toward the hag's form, so the party debates whether she is "going evil." Eldil casts detect evil and good and finds she isn't fully fey, but something fey is taking root in her — she is in transition.
  • [0:08:52] They try to halt the change: dispel magic (no effect), holy water, and protection from evil and good — nothing stops it.
  • [0:12:39] Grimgil walks into the forest and ritual-casts divination — cinnamon burned, his blood poured into the earth — asking whether Clara is an evil threat. The omen is cryptic: she is becoming a hag, but "even the incarnation of evil isn't necessarily a threat to you."
  • [0:20:39] The party weighs killing and revivifying her to strip the curse versus letting her live. They note that slaying Old Mother Salt already lifted the swamp's illusion and that the neck-marks and curses she left them are fading.
  • [0:25:59] Questioned about her plans, Clara dreams of a normal life — a house, a bakery — but insists the hag isn't truly dead and expects to be caged again soon; Jaden conjures a minor illusion of the hag to test her reaction.
  • [0:32:04] Eldil casts remove curse on Clara. Between that and the morning's efforts her form settles back toward normal and the fogginess leaves her, though the party stays wary.
  • [0:35:06] Jaden ritual-casts animal messenger, sending a sparrow with a letter to his father Gustave.
  • [0:39:09] With the illusion gone the forest is far smaller than remembered. Bow recalls the local geography and the party settles on Springvale, Bow's hometown, to leave the rescued children rather than Oakhurst or Bruxar; Grimgil scouts from the air to find the fastest way out of the woods.
  • [1:01:16] Two days' travel later they near Springvale. Bow is sent ahead as a sparrow to scout: the town is guarded but there are no wanted posters of him on display and no imminent danger.
  • [1:08:13] At the inn, the Grubby Griffin, dwarf innkeeper Roderick Stonehand recognizes Bow. He reveals that Bow's father Therian was taken by the Royal Guard about a year ago and the family farmhouse has sat empty since — and that the guards were hunting Bow too.
  • [1:21:26] Roderick sends them to the Caldwell farm to house the children. Mrs. Caldwell takes them in, and the party leaves the kids plus 50 gold; they keep Clara to rest at the inn. The Caldwells mention the king has been demanding extra rations from the small towns.
  • [1:29:48] Bow visits his father's ransacked house and recounts his last memory: mercenaries and Royal Guard came, Therian told him to run, and he fell unconscious and woke near the party. Grimgil magically repairs the house with stone shape and controlled flame.
  • [1:44:50] Grimgil ritual-casts divination again: Bow's father is alive and can be found.
  • [1:53:10] Grimgil casts plant growth over the Caldwell farmland (an eight-hour ritual) to double the year's harvest, helping the village meet the king's rations; Bow restocks metal-shafted arrows at the forge.
  • [1:58:41] Reading the hag's shipping papers, the party finds a ledger of crates of children bound for Caldaren (the largest shipment), Drusburgh, and Grey Harbour, with coded dates — evidence of a child-trafficking network.
  • [2:00:31] A letter from Therian hidden in Bow's pack, read alongside the papers, reveals that Bow is the illegitimate son of King Aldric Stormborn, secretly raised on the farm on the king's orders by Therian, a high-ranking army officer. The king has grown paranoid — "whispers into corridors grow louder... he sees threats and shadows that may not even exist, and I have become one of those shadows for him" — and now wants Bow dead or alive, possibly tied to The Absolute. The party resolves to investigate Grey Harbour and eventually return to Baldur’s Gate.
  • [2:22:00] That night the party keeps watch in the repaired house. On his watch Bow hears screaming from the neighboring Caldwell farm.
  • [2:29:22] Grimgil polymorphs into a giant eagle and the party flies over: Mrs. Caldwell lies unconscious on the porch, and inside they find Alden Caldwell transformed into a mind flayer and feeding, while the rescued children are being infected and turning too. Initiative is rolled. To be continued.
