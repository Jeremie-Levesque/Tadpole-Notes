Session 17

  • The party is at the gates of Frila, the guards tells the party they should talk to Vallen Thorn to get information on the necromancer. The party heads towards city hall to talk to the investigators, the city looks destroyed, most homes are in disrepair, some are boarded up.

Vallen Thorn does not Believe Eldil, informs the party that Astarion has been helping them for the few weeks. Bow asks him about the other problems in town, but he is unwilling to share any details after the ordeal. Eldil needs to stay in the cell for the night.

Astarion asks if the party came from the ship, he explains that he was also from the ship, he ran straight to Frila. Bow compares Astarion to Eldil, Astarion says he has a gift for him. Astarion has been Cazador's prisoner for a while. he was freed since he has had the tadpole implanted in him on the ship.

The party talks to Halsin, he tells the party that Malkavor Ardin was just an ugly poor boy that couldn't get into a magic school, he had inate magic and demonstrated sociapathic tendencies, his mother was down with the sickness and the necromancer lost it and is holding the town accountable because they didn't help her at all. Vallen Thorn admitted that they intentionally didn't provide aid to him because they feared him. In his final hint, he revealed that the mayor souls was the last piece needed to unleash permanent death on frila.
  • While Eldil and Jaden are talking about Eldil's anger issues, Astarion drops in and asks him why he's been so aggro, Astarion explains that his undead stain is a curse, a curse he accepted while on death's door that turned him into a thrall for 1000 years. Eldil gives Jaden's his axe and tries to approach him, Astarion gives him a parting word "Do not accept help from Cazador". Astarion also offers to find a way off the island, Eldil seems excited about that prospect.
  • The party heads out of Frila to the Crypt, there are beings in the mists walking around they are zombies (possibly doing the thriller dance), the party tries to stealth past the zombies to get to the crypt, Eldil's armor clinks about. Roll for initative.
